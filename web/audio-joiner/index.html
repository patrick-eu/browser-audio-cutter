    How to merge audio files online

    1. Choose two or more audio files. You can add more later.
    2. Drag the clips into the order you want.
    3. Set a fade in or fade out for any clip, and a crossfade between clips if you want smooth changes.
    4. Press Preview merged track to listen.
    5. Pick MP3 or WAV and press Merge and download.

    Audio joiner FAQ

    Are my files uploaded to combine them?

    No. This audio merger decodes, joins and encodes the files inside your browser. The only files your browser downloads are this page and its two scripts, all from this site.

    Can I use it as an MP3 joiner?

    Yes. Add MP3 files, put them in order and export the combined audio as one MP3 at 128, 192, 256 or 320 kbps, or as WAV. You can also mix MP3 and WAV clips in one join.

    What does the exported file sound like?

    It is written at 44.1 kHz. If any clip is stereo, the result is stereo and mono clips play in both channels; if every clip is mono, the result is mono. With WAV and no fades or crossfade, the clips follow each other sample for sample. MP3 encoding adds a short stretch of silence at the very start of the file.

    Is there a limit on files or length?

    This page sets none. All decoded clips are kept in memory while you work, so many or long files need more memory and the practical limit depends on your device.
